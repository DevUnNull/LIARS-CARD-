PHAN I - CAC LUONG BE4 DA CO
============================

BE4-01 - XAC DINH VA TINH HINH PHAT (PENALTY)
---------------------------------------------
Muc dich:
- Backend la nguon quyet dinh dung/sai cua challenge, nguoi bi phat, ket qua
  revolver, song/chet.
- Unity khong gui ket qua penalty hoac trang thai song/chet de server tin theo.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Client gui challenge kem matchId, actionId, expectedVersion, targetPlayId.
2. DeckAction/Challenge flow xac thuc player tu JWT va kiem tra thanh vien tran,
   phase, luot, phien ban, play con hop le.
3. PenaltyService kiem tra du lieu last play va tinh bluff qua DeckRuleEngine.
4. PenaltyService chon nguoi bi phat theo ket qua challenge, kiem tra revolver va
   server tu pull trigger.
5. Neu hit, backend danh dau player IsAlive=false va xoa hand; neu khong hit,
   player song. Client chi nhan ket qua can cho UI.
6. Backend dem AlivePlayers tu GameMatch. Khi con toi da mot nguoi, dat phase
   MatchEnded, winner la nguoi song duy nhat hoac null neu khong co ai.

Quy tac an toan:
- Khong gui vi tri dan/buồng dan noi bo hoac logic an toan cua revolver xuong client.
- Khong nhan winner/IsAlive do client khai bao.
- Request trung duoc xu ly bang actionId + payload hash trong MatchStore.

BE4-02 - ACK/THOI HAN ANIMATION PENALTY
---------------------------------------
Muc dich:
- SignalR phat penalty animation start den nhom match.
- Client ACK chi khi animation chinh da hien thi xong.
- ACK khong thay doi penalty result, alive state, winner, round hay phase gameplay.

Luong hoat dong:
1. ChallengeService tao PenaltyEventId va PenaltyAnimationSession.
2. Session ghi danh sach player can ACK (roster cua tran), trang thai Pending,
   thoi han server va so ACK.
3. Server phat PenaltyAnimationStarted qua SignalR hub.
4. Hub lay player identity tu ket noi da xac thuc; khong dung playerId tu payload.
5. ACK hop le duoc ghi trong session. ACK du, hoac timeout server (mac dinh 30
   giay, co the chinh qua PenaltyAnimation.AckTimeoutSeconds), se ket thuc session.
6. ACK duplicate, sai event, outsider hoac ACK sau terminal khong lap side effect.
7. Hosted timeout worker quet session pending; reconnect snapshot co the cung cap
   session dang pending va terminal gan nhat cho client.

Luu y:
- Session ACK va MatchStore dang o RAM, khong persistence qua server restart.
- Unity chua tich hop SignalR client; gameplay UI tiep tuc dong bo qua REST.

BE4-03 - ELIMINATE PLAYER & DETERMINE WINNER
--------------------------------------------
Muc dich:
- Ket qua penalty cap nhat trang thai loai nguoi va ket thuc tran o Backend.
- Client chi cap nhat UI tu snapshot/event authoritative.

Luong hoat dong:
1. PenaltyService danh dau IsAlive=false va xoa hand neu player bi eliminate.
2. Backend dem AlivePlayers tu state match.
3. Neu con mot nguoi song: MatchEnded va WinnerPlayerId la player do.
4. Neu khong con ai song: MatchEnded va WinnerPlayerId=null; khong tu tao winner.
5. ChallengeService luu match/action duoi lock theo match; sau khi lock duoc nha,
   phat PlayerEliminated va/hoac MatchEnded.
6. Public state snapshot co Players, Phase, WinnerPlayerId, StateVersion va
   currentPlayerId cho Unity xac dinh player local.
7. Unity loc snapshot cu, dedupe UI notices va chan play/challenge local khi player
   chet, tran ket thuc, khong phai luot hoac chua co state authoritative.

BE4-04 - POST-PENALTY ORCHESTRATION / START NEXT ROUND ONCE
----------------------------------------------------------
Muc dich:
- Sau penalty, chi mot diem duyet state de quyet dinh start round tiep hay ket thuc.

Luong hoat dong:
1. PenaltyService ket luan penalty/elimination/winner; khong tu goi StartRound.
2. ChallengeService dang giu lock match, xem phase/winner/AlivePlayers.
3. Neu tran khong ket thuc, winner null va con hon mot nguoi song, goi
   DeckRoundService.StartRound dung mot lan.
4. Save match va action result, sau do nha lock.
5. Phat RoundStarted sau commit; event co PenaltyEventId, RoundNumber,
   StateVersion, phase, table rank, current turn.
6. Action retry dung actionId lay cached result, khong chia lai bai/tao vong moi.
7. SetReady chi start vong ban dau o WaitingForPlayers; khong mo lai tran
   MatchEnded. ACK/timeout khong goi StartRound.

Vi tri StartRound:
- ChallengeService: round moi sau penalty neu tran tiep tuc.
- MatchService: vong dau khi roster san sang va phase WaitingForPlayers.
- DeckRoundService: noi thuc thi chia bai/reset vong, khong tu phat event.

BE4-05 - PERSIST & QUERY MATCH RESULTS
--------------------------------------
Muc dich:
- Chi luu snapshot ket qua khi state authoritative da la MatchEnded.
- MatchStore gameplay van o RAM; MongoDB chi giu ket qua tran da ket thuc.

Schema Mongo:
- Collection: match_results.
- Unique index: ux_match_results_match_id tren matchId (string).
- Document: matchId, winnerPlayerId nullable, players[{playerId, displayName,
  seatIndex, isAliveAtEnd, isWinner, remainingCardCountAtEnd}],
  matchCreatedAtUtc, endedAtUtc, finalStateVersion.
- Khong luu hand, card ID, revolver/bullet, token hoac secret gameplay.
- GameMatch khong co StartedAt/EndedAt rieng; khong gia lap StartedAt.

Luong luu:
1. ChallengeService ket thuc gameplay va cache action result trong MatchStore.
2. Nhả lock, MatchResultService lay snapshot tu state authoritative; service chi
   chap nhan Phase=MatchEnded.
3. Insert document vao Mongo; unique matchId chong insert trung.
4. Neu duplicate key, doc document da co:
   - Ket qua giong nhau: retry thanh cong, giu nguyen document/timestamp.
   - Ket qua khac: log conflict co matchId/version, tra loi conflict, khong update.
5. Chi sau persist thanh cong moi phat MatchEnded va tra response thanh cong.
6. Neu Mongo loi, tra 503; retry action da cache thu lai persistence, khong chay
   lai penalty/elimination/StartRound. MatchEnded event co marker trong RAM de
   tranh phat nghiep vu trung lap trong cung process.

Truy van:
- GET /api/matches/{matchId}/result.
- JWT bat buoc. Chi participant co trong result document duoc xem.
- 200 participant + co document; 404 neu chua co result hoac khong phai participant;
  401 neu chua xac thuc; loi Mongo tra 503.
- DTO public khong tra truc tiep Mongo document.
- Unity goi REST endpoint khi snapshot gameplay bao MatchEnded. UI phan biet
  MatchEnded voi SavedResultLoaded, hien loading/not found/error/retry.

Van hanh Mongo:
- Connection/database qua MongoDbContext trong appsettings/environment secrets.
- Application tao unique index matchId luc startup.
- Integration test co the chay voi Mongo local; khong nham test database voi
  database that. Khong drop collection/database san pham.

Gioi han recovery:
- MatchStore van o RAM. Neu server restart truoc khi state ket thuc duoc ghi
  Mongo, backend khong co du lieu ben vung de tai tao tran do.
- Khong co Mongo transaction giua MatchStore va result; khong tuyen bo exactly-once
  tren ca hai he thong. Unique key va cached retry bao ve cac truong hop retry khi
  process con state.


PHAN II - VAN HANH LUONG GAMEPLAY END-TO-END
============================================

1. Tao match, roster va ready duoc xu ly boi backend. MatchStore giu GameMatch,
   player hands va revolver state trong RAM.
2. Backend chia vong dau, chon table rank/current turn; client doc state qua REST.
3. Player danh bai qua REST; backend xac thuc danh tinh, validate turn/version,
   va giu last play trong state.
4. Player khac challenge. Backend tu tinh bluff, penalty va revolver.
5. Neu penalty tiep tuc tran: backend start round mot lan, commit state,
   phat elimination/round event neu can; client resync snapshot va hand qua REST.
6. Neu penalty ket thuc tran: backend xac dinh winner/null, commit state va action,
   persist result Mongo, sau do phat MatchEnded.
7. Penalty animation ACK/timeout co the dien ra doc lap; no khong gate gameplay,
   khong doi winner va khong tao vong.
8. Unity hien thi state/result authoritative. UI ket thuc tran khong khang dinh
   result da duoc luu cho den khi GET result thanh cong.

Xu ly van hanh / kiem tra:
- Backend: dotnet build Back_End.csproj --no-restore
- Unit + Mongo integration test:
  PowerShell: $env:RUN_MATCH_RESULTS_MONGO_INTEGRATION='1';
              dotnet test Tests/Back_End.Tests.csproj

  ngau nhien va drop dung database do test tao.

- Khong commit/push khi chua duoc yeu cau.

BE6-03 - LICH SU DAU
--------------------
Muc tieu:
Tra danh sach tran da hoan tat ma user hien tai la participant, co phan trang.

API de xuat:
GET /api/matches/history?limit=20&cursor={opaqueCursor}

Quy tac:
- playerId lay tu claim JWT, khong nhan playerId tuy y tu client.
- Query filter players.playerId == current user.
- Sap xep endedAtUtc giam dan, sau do matchId giam/tang theo mot quy uoc co dinh
  de tie-break timestamp.
- Limit mac dinh 20, gioi han toi da de xuat 100.
- Cursor opaque encode endedAtUtc + matchId; cursor trang tiep theo dung keyset
  pagination, tranh skip du lieu khi tran moi chen vao.
- DTO tra matchId, winner, endedAtUtc, thong tin participant can thiet va cursor
  tiep theo; khong tra secret fields.
- Khong tim thay tran rieng le co the bo qua; danh sach rong la HTTP 200 voi items=[].

Index Mongo de xuat:
- Multikey index tren players.playerId va endedAtUtc; them matchId de ho tro sort
  tie-break neu Mongo query planner chap nhan index pattern.
- Xac minh explain/query plan voi Mongo version va schema thuc te truoc deploy.

Unity:
- Them man history/list neu UX duoc duyet; load page, loading/error/empty/retry.
- Goi endpoint bang access token; khong gui playerId.

BE6-04 - THONG KE NGUOI CHOI
----------------------------
Muc tieu:
Tinh thong ke tu ket qua cuoi Backend, khong cong lap cung mot tran.

API de xuat:
GET /api/players/me/statistics

Metric co the tinh tu schema hien tai:
- completedMatchCount: so document co player trong players.
- winCount: winnerPlayerId == playerId.
- lossCount: winnerPlayerId != null va winnerPlayerId != playerId.
- noWinnerCount: winnerPlayerId == null.
- winRate: can chot mau so truoc khi code.

De xuat nghiep vu can duyet:
- Tinh tran khong winner la no-contest: van tinh vao completedMatchCount/noWinnerCount,
  nhung khong tinh vao win/loss denominator.
- winRate = winCount / (winCount + lossCount); neu mau so = 0, tra null hoac 0
  theo contract UI duoc nhom chon.
- Khong dung remaining cards hay so lan penalty lam metric neu chua co yeu cau.
- Unique matchId trong collection giup aggregation moi tran toi da mot dong.
- Query aggregate tren match_results; co the tinh on-demand luc dau.
- Neu luu cache/denormalized stats sau nay, can co quy trinh rebuild tu match_results
  va idempotency theo matchId; khong cong incremental theo event co the phat lap.

BE6-05 - BANG XEP HANG
----------------------
Muc tieu:
Xep hang nguoi choi theo tieu chi nhom da thong nhat; phan trang va tie-break ro.

Can nhom nghiep vu quyet dinh truoc khi implement:
1. Metric chinh la so tran thang, win rate, hay diem leaderboard?
2. Tran no-winner co bi loai khoi mau so khong?
3. So tran toi thieu de xuat hien tren bang?
4. Tie-break theo win count, completed matches, hay thoi diem dat thanh tich?
5. Cung tat ca metric bang nhau thi dong hang (rank=1,1,3) hay sap tiep theo
   playerId/displayName chi de on dinh thu tu?

Khong tu chon mot trong cac phuong an tren thay cho owner.

Pipeline de xuat sau khi chot quy tac:
1. Unwind players trong match_results.
2. Group theo players.playerId, tinh completed/wins/losses/noWinner theo quy tac
   BE6-04 va tinh metric xep hang duoc duyet.
3. Sap xep theo metric chinh giam dan, sau do cac tie-break da duyet.
4. Them playerId lam tie-break cuoi cung de pagination deterministic neu quy tac
   dong hang yeu cau thu tu on dinh; phan biet thu tu hien thi voi competition rank.
5. Cursor pagination encode toan bo sort tuple; khong chi cursor theo score neu
   nhieu nguoi bang diem.
6. Gioi han page size, response co items va nextCursor.

Cache:
- Chua can cache trong ban dau neu quy mo nho.
- Neu can cache, invalidate/recompute tu persisted match result; khong xem event
  SignalR la nguon chinh va khong cap nhat bang cache theo event khong idempotent.
